About Mappa Mundi

An interactive geography learning game for knowing the world by heart.

Mappa Mundi is an interactive geography game for learning the world map through short, focused practice. Students build map knowledge by working directly with countries, continents, oceans, U.S. states, capitals, and world regions.

Instead of memorizing places from a list, students see them on the map, hear the names, place labels, review mistakes, and gradually build a stronger mental picture of the world.

Over 20 learning journeys

Mappa Mundi includes over 20 unique journeys, with guided practice for continents and oceans, U.S. states, capitals, world regions, and countries around the globe.

The app currently covers 122 countries, with new features and new content added once a week or more.

Each journey is broken into short activities so students can make steady progress without being overwhelmed by the whole map at once.

Practice on the map

Study first, then challenge yourself

Mappa Mundi is built around a simple learning flow: study the map, practice the places, then test what you remember.

Students can use study mode, challenge mode, Memory Trail, and Daily Trail to build lasting recall over time. The goal is not just to get one quiz right. The goal is to know the world by heart.

Built for families, classrooms, and curious learners

Mappa Mundi works well for homeschool families, classrooms, and independent learners who want geography practice that feels more active than a worksheet and more thoughtful than a basic multiple-choice quiz.

It is simple enough for kids to start quickly, but broad enough to support steady geography learning as new regions and activities are added.
