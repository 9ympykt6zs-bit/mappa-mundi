# Mappa Mundi Vision

## What Mappa Mundi Is

Mappa Mundi is a geography learning app. It will eventually become a customizable, interactive, adaptive, playable atlas.

It will teach users content that includes, but is not limited to:

- all countries recognized by the United Nations;
- relevant divisions of those countries, such as states, provinces, territories, and important regions;
- important cities;
- physical features such as mountain ranges, rivers, lakes, trails, seas, and deserts;
- important natural resource deposits;
- historic maps;
- and other significant items that might traditionally appear in an atlas.

Mappa Mundi will teach more than the physical location of these items. It will help users understand why they are relevant, what surrounds them, and how they relate to other geographic, historical, cultural, and physical features.

## Learning Experience

Mappa Mundi will teach geography through a combination of interactive map use, spaced repetition, quizzes, puzzles, games, and other learning activities.

Repeated retrieval and spatial interaction will help users build durable geographic knowledge. At the same time, the app will go beyond memorization by helping learners understand the significance of places and features and the relationships among them.

A successful Mappa Mundi learner should not merely recognize names on a map. They should develop a durable mental model of the world: where places are, what surrounds them, what physical features shape them, why they matter, and how geographic features relate to history, culture, resources, and one another.

Game mechanics should serve learning and exploration rather than becoming the primary purpose of the product.

## Customization

Mappa Mundi will be highly customizable.

A student preparing for a geography test should be able to limit the app to exactly the material they need to study. A teacher or homeschool parent should be able to align the app with the scope and sequence of a particular curriculum.

Users who want a broader experience should be able to explore progressively larger bodies of geographic knowledge without having to configure the app manually.

The adaptive learning system should respond to each learner's strengths, weaknesses, prior knowledge, and progress so that review and new material are presented appropriately.

## Exploration

Mappa Mundi will also provide a rich and beautiful experience for users who simply want to explore the world.

The map should be useful even outside a game or structured lesson. Users should be able to move through the atlas freely, investigate geographic features, discover relationships among places, and learn from the map through curiosity.

Over time, Mappa Mundi should include animated lessons and other interactive explanations about geographic features and concepts.

An AI assistant should allow users to ask questions about what they are seeing and receive useful explanations within the context of the map.

## Audience

Mappa Mundi is intended for anyone interested in learning geography, from children encountering geography for the first time to adults who want to deepen or refresh their knowledge.

The experience should be approachable enough for younger learners while containing enough depth to remain useful and interesting to older students and adults.

## Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal is for Mappa Mundi to become a comprehensive interactive atlas in which learning, exploration, and play reinforce one another.

A user should be able to begin with a narrow objective—such as learning the states of the United States—and progressively build toward a detailed understanding of the world.

Mappa Mundi should make geographic knowledge easier to acquire, easier to retain, and more meaningful by connecting locations on a map with the physical, historical, cultural, and human relationships that make those places important.
