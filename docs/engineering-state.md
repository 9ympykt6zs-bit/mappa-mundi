## Verification and risks

- Guided capital answer banks: U.S. political naming prompts keep their existing session-scoped choices when four valid options are available, then use same-category catalog targets only to fill a short bank. Restoring an answering prompt repairs missing, duplicate, unlabeled, or undersized banks while preserving a valid saved bank and its order. A catalog fallback miss retains the selected label for correction feedback but records progress and canonical evidence only against the active curriculum target. The combined fast baseline passed **113/113**; the new restoration/evidence browser coverage passed **6/6** across desktop and mobile, and established capital marker/retrieval coverage passed **26/26**.
- Reconstruction snap integrity: an accepted drag writes the exact canonical piece position synchronously before its short visual snap animation begins. Animation is presentation-only, so navigation, teardown, persistence, or an interrupted frame cannot preserve the accepted piece at its approximate drop coordinate. The existing centralized 32 CSS px mouse/trackpad and 40 CSS px touch tolerances are unchanged.
- U.S. critical path: `npm run test:critical-path` is the required compact browser gate before meaningful application changes are pushed to `main`, after feature-specific checks and before `git diff --check`. Tagged scenarios cover Reconstruction, map navigation, capital learning, physical geography, and Guided progression without replacing the full fast or affected browser suites. The initial accepted gate passed **14/14** in about **2.3 minutes**; the full fast baseline passed **113/113**. See [testing](testing.md).

- Cursor-anchored map zoom: MapLibre 5.18 already owns wheel, trackpad, and pinch zoom and provides exact cursor anchoring at regional zooms. Its low-zoom globe helper intentionally blends toward a center-based heuristic whenever the globe is small, even for points well inside the visible surface. The shared runner now reapplies MapLibre's own `setLocationAtPoint` operation below zoom 3 when the pointer lies safely inside the globe horizon and its longitude is within a conservative 70 degrees of the camera center. MapLibre retains its native safety fallback near the horizon and for farther points. Predominantly horizontal non-pinch wheel input still pans; `ctrlKey` pinch-style wheel input remains with MapLibre. This is a narrow compatibility shim around MapLibre 5.18 internals and must be re-audited when changing MapLibre versions. The fast baseline passed **112/112**; focused desktop cursor/navigation/Guided camera acceptance passed **26/26**, and a real emulated two-finger mobile pinch passed **1/1**. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Reconstruction release tolerance: regional, Guided, standalone, and Lower 48 drag paths share `MAP_RECONSTRUCTION_PLACEMENT_TOLERANCE`. A single-piece release measures the canonical translation delta through the active SVG screen matrix, making the 32 CSS-pixel mouse/trackpad and 40 CSS-pixel touch limits stable across viewport size, pan, and zoom. The boundary is inclusive; an accepted piece snaps to the exact canonical translation. Multi-piece moves and releases outside the limit retain their existing positions and retry/submission behavior. Guided anchored scoring, standalone global translation normalization, Lower 48 evaluation, evidence, navigation, and persistence remain unchanged. The fast baseline passed **112/112**; affected Reconstruction/Guided browser acceptance passed **16 applicable cases** with **2 intentional pointer-project skips**, and focused Lower 48 persistence/lifecycle coverage passed **3 applicable cases** with **1 intentional mobile skip**. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Reconstruction lifecycle ownership: the persistent `#map-reconstruction-panel` is no longer allowed to outlive the `map-reconstruction` app screen. All runtime screen transitions pass through one setter that destroys the regional or Lower 48 controller, clears panel DOM and mode classes, restores the map accessibility state, and drops only the live Guided block reference. Durable Guided child contracts and Lower 48 placements remain available for explicit resume and do not control visibility. Pointer cleanup releases captures, restores the pre-drag Lower 48 session on interruption, blocks wheel camera changes during piece gestures, and cancels interactions on `pagehide`. The new lifecycle matrix passed **14/14 applicable desktop/mobile cases** with **2 expected mobile skips** for desktop mouse gestures; the fast baseline passed **112/112** and established Reconstruction navigation passed **4/4**. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Guided completion now derives from the required curriculum rather than the old 50-state-only boundary: 50 state and 50 capital introductions, ten nonrepeatable Reconstruction checkpoints, and 34 physical introductions plus their 34 immediate practice completions. Mastery, optional Connections, repeatable review, post-course Reconstruction, and deferred content remain excluded. The capstone asks one fixed persisted set of ten previously introduced targets: four states, three capitals, and one river, lake, and mountain range. Incorrect answers count as attempts and emit normal canonical evidence; reload preserves order and response progress. The post-course chooser appears only after question ten. The fast baseline passed **112/112**; the comprehensive affected browser matrix passed **96/98**, exposed a pre-existing Journey-evidence variable-shadowing defect, and its repaired cases passed **2/2**. Additional stale/isolated release checks passed **12/12** after their assertions were aligned with current UI and inert-context behavior. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Guided physical retrieval renders the complete supported inventory: 8 rivers, 6 lakes, and 20 mountain ranges. The synthetic retrieval activity uses all 34 targets as neutral, unlabeled, hit-testable map context while the Memory Trail pool, checkpoint, review scheduling, mastery, and canonical evidence retain only bounded introduced question targets. Lower-48 retrieval fits the 32 non-Alaska context features; Alaska-only questions retain the regional preset. Manual pan/zoom remains available. Contract version 4 repairs legacy candidate-only or one-target child records on reload. The existing source assets are already loaded and no additional network payload was introduced. The fast baseline passed **111/111**, Guided orchestration passed **24/24**, and the final physical-presentation desktop/mobile run passed **32/32**. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Guided highlighted-feature teaching: states, mountain ranges, rivers, and lakes now show a family instruction, name and highlight one target, and explicitly request a tap. The spoken family instruction precedes target names and is deduplicated within the active cohort; an interrupted cohort resumes from its persisted target with synchronized visible/audio cues. Wrong teaching taps only redirect and do not create incorrect evidence, while correct taps retain canonical `assisted` semantics and immediate focused retrieval. The fast baseline passed **111/111** checks and the selected desktop/mobile Guided browser matrix passed **18/18**. Details are in [testing](testing.md).

- Guided Reconstruction eligibility: a Guided-only reset clears the authoritative `introducedItemIds` gate while preserving canonical learning history. Each checkpoint requires all of its own `state:<id>` introductions plus the prior checkpoint completion chain. Incomplete child contracts are revalidated on reload.
